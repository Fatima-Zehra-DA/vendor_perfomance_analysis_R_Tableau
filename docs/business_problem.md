## Background
Effective inventory and sales management are critical for optimizing profitability in the retail and wholesale industry. 
Organizations often face losses due to inefficient pricing strategies, suboptimal inventory turnover, and excessive dependency on a limited set of vendors. 
Understanding vendor performance and its impact on sales, costs, and profitability is essential for informed decision-making.

## Problem Statement
The organization lacks clear visibility into vendor-level performance across purchases, sales, and profitability. 
Without structured analysis, it is difficult to identify underperforming vendors, optimize pricing strategies, and assess the financial impact of purchasing decisions.

## Objectives
The primary objective of this analysis is to evaluate vendor performance using transactional purchase, sales, pricing, and invoice data in order to support strategic decision-making.

Specifically, this project aims to:

- Identify underperforming brands and vendors that may require pricing or promotional adjustments
- Determine top-performing vendors based on sales contribution and gross profit
- Analyze the impact of bulk purchasing on unit costs and vendor efficiency
- Assess inventory turnover to identify opportunities to reduce holding costs and improve operational efficiency
- Compare profitability variance between high-performing and low-performing vendors

## Analytical Scope
- The analysis focuses on vendor-level performance using purchase, sales, pricing, and invoice data
- Inventory datasets are included to support optional turnover and efficiency analysis
- The project emphasizes descriptive and diagnostic analytics rather than predictive modeling

## Expected Outcomes
This analysis is expected to provide actionable insights into vendor performance, cost efficiency, and profitability drivers. 
The results can be used by business stakeholders to improve vendor negotiations, optimize purchasing strategies, and enhance overall financial performance.

## Stakeholders
- Procurement and purchasing teams
- Finance and accounting teams
- Inventory and operations management
- Business leadership and decision-makers
